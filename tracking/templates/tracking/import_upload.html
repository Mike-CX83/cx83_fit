Prepare your file

This accepts the dashboard's measurement format. Files from MyFitnessPal, Oura or Apple Health need their own supported import.

Use the same source name and stable row IDs when importing again. Matching rows are skipped. Changed values under an existing ID or previously deleted rows block the entire import.

Required columns: source_record_id, metric, value, unit, local_date, source_timezone, synthetic.

Optional columns: side, location, notes, observed_at.

Format details

Use a date such as 2026-11-06, an IANA timezone such as America/Chicago, and true or false for synthetic. Synthetic rows stay excluded from personal history.

Weight uses kg or lb. Circumferences use cm or in and require a location. Arm, thigh and calf also require left or right. An optional observed time must include its UTC offset.
